- A new update window, in the style of a macOS alert, shows each step of an update.
- MonoPods can check for updates automatically, about once a day. It asks once at first launch, and you can change this at any time with Automatic Updates in Options.
- MonoPods is available in eight languages: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese and Simplified Chinese. It follows the language of your Mac.
- The version line shows the version without the build number.
- The About window is in English in every language.
- The Buy Me a Coffee button in the About window has a capsule shape.
- MonoPods is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
- macOS may ask once more for Bluetooth access after this update.
